## Introduction
Hello! I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group** (Hong Kong University of Science and Technology). My core research areas are **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** and **Machine Learning**, with focused interests in large language model reasoning, reinforcement learning for LLMs, hallucination mitigation in vision-language models (VLMs), and the truthfulness & interpretability of large language models.

## Academic Background

## Research Interests
My current research focuses on three main directions:
1. LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
2. Hallucination Detection & Mitigation in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
3. LLM Truthfulness and Inner Model Interpretability
